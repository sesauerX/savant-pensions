Article IV: Retirement Income Plan

4.1
Establishment. The Company maintains the {{company_name}} Retirement Income Plan (the “Plan”) as set out in the Company’s Corporate Pension Policy and Retirement Income Plan. The Plan is a private retirement benefit arrangement of the Company. It is not intended to be a qualified retirement plan under the laws of the United States.
4.2
Awards. The Company may award pensions under the Plan by written consent of the Member.
4.3
Vested and irrevocable. Once awarded, a pension, together with any survivor pension attached to it, is fully vested, non-forfeitable and irrevocable, and is an obligation of the Company for the lifetime of each person entitled to it.
4.4
No reduction or cancellation. Neither the Member nor any manager, officer, future member or future owner of the Company may cancel, reduce, suspend or otherwise impair an awarded pension.
4.5
Binding on successors. Obligations under awarded pensions bind the Company and its successors and assigns, and survive:
(a)
any transfer of membership interests, including a transfer on the death of the Member to the Member’s heirs{{#if successors}}, namely {{successors}}{{/if}};
(b)
any change of management;
(c)
any merger, conversion or reorganization of the Company; and
(d)
any sale or transfer of all or substantially all of the Company’s assets, in which case the acquirer assumes the obligations, or the Company secures them in full before the transfer.
4.6
Dissolution. On dissolution, the Company shall settle or fully secure all obligations under awarded pensions before any distribution to members.
4.7
Designated Fund. The Company maintains a separate account in its own name, used solely to meet its obligations under the Plan (the “Designated Fund”). The Designated Fund is the primary source of pension payments. The Company’s other assets remain available to meet any shortfall.
4.8
Amendment. This Article IV may be amended only with the written consent of each person entitled to an awarded pension and of the Plan Administrator. No amendment may reduce an awarded pension.

5.1

5.2
Powers. The Plan Administrator, acting through its authorized officers, is authorized for and on behalf of the Company to:
(a)
administer the Plan and the Designated Fund, including making the monthly pension payments and the annual adjustments;
(b)
execute, certify and attest pension-related documents of the Company, including award letters, certificates and true copies of Company documents and resolutions; and
(c)
act as authorized signatory of the Company toward financial institutions, government authorities and immigration authorities in all matters concerning the Plan.
5.3
Replacement. The Member may appoint a replacement Plan Administrator by written notice. A replacement does not affect any awarded pension.
5.4
Reliance. Any third party may rely on a document signed by the Plan Administrator under this Article V as a valid act of the Company.
