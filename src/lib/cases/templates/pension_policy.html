Section 1: Establishment

1.1
This Policy establishes the {{company_name}} Retirement Income Plan (the “Plan”) under Article IV of the Company’s Operating Agreement. It is effective on {{signing_date}}.
1.2
The Plan is a private retirement benefit arrangement of {{company_name}} (the “Company”). It is not intended to be a qualified retirement plan under the laws of the United States.

Section 2: Definitions

  • Award: a pension granted under the Plan by written consent of the Company’s member.
  • Beneficiary: the person to whom a pension is awarded.
  • Survivor: the one person, the Beneficiary’s spouse or partner, named in an Award to receive a survivor pension after the Beneficiary’s death.
  • Designated Fund: the separate account in the Company’s name used solely to meet the Company’s obligations under the Plan.
  • Plan Administrator: Sterling Pension Group LLC, 15 N Main St #100, West Hartford, CT 06107, USA, or its successor appointed under the Operating Agreement.

Section 3: Eligibility

The Company may make Awards to its members, managers, officers, employees and consultants in recognition of their service to the Company.

Section 4: Benefits

4.1
Lifetime pension. An Award provides a monthly pension in US dollars, payable for the remainder of the Beneficiary’s life, starting on the commencement date stated in the Award.
4.2
Survivor pension. Where an Award names a Survivor, payments continue without interruption after the Beneficiary’s death to the Survivor for the remainder of the Survivor’s life, at the full monthly amount then payable, with continuing annual adjustments. A Survivor is optional, only one Survivor may be named, and the Survivor is fixed when the Award is made.

Section 5: Annual adjustment

On {{adjustment_date}} of each year after the first payment, the monthly amount increases by the percentage change in the {{adjustment_index}} over the preceding calendar year. If that change is negative, the monthly amount stays unchanged.

Section 6: Vesting

Every Award is fully vested, non-forfeitable and irrevocable from the date it is made.

Section 7: Funding

The Company maintains the Designated Fund, administered by the Plan Administrator, as the primary source of pension payments. The Company’s other assets remain available to meet any shortfall. The Plan Administrator monitors whether the Designated Fund is sufficient to meet the Company’s obligations under the Plan.

Section 8: Payment

Pensions are paid monthly by bank transfer from the Designated Fund to an account held in the name of the Beneficiary or Survivor.

Section 9: Administration

The Plan Administrator keeps the records of the Plan, makes the payments and annual adjustments, and issues certificates, award letters and affidavits for reliance by third parties, including banks and other financial institutions, consulates and immigration authorities.

Section 10: Amendment and termination

The Plan may be amended or terminated only with effect for the future. No amendment or termination may reduce an Award already made. On termination, the Company settles or fully secures all Awards already made.

Section 11: Successors

The obligations of the Company under the Plan bind its successors and assigns, as set out in Article IV of the Operating Agreement.
