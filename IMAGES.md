# Image slots

All site imagery was generated with **OpenAI GPT Image 2** (`openai/gpt-image-2` on Replicate, quality `high`,
2048×1152 for 16:9 and 21:9 slots, 1536×1152 for 4:3), reviewed by eye, and exported as responsive WebP by
cropping to the slot's aspect ratio. Responsive files live in `public/images/site/` and
`public/images/briefing/`; `src/lib/images.json` maps each slot to `src`, `srcset`, size and alt text, and
`src/components/Pic.astro` renders it. Post covers also keep a 1600×900 JPG at the old URL
(`/images/briefing/YYYY-MM-DD.jpg`) because `og:image` points there.

Hero photos sit under a navy overlay that darkens the left, so subjects are in the right third.

  Replace it with a real team photo when one exists.

  photo of the West Hartford office here; swap it in when the client supplies one.
- The default Open Graph image `/og.jpg` is unchanged.
