# Sterling case admin: operator guide

Sign in at **sterlingpensiongroup.com/admin** with your email address. You get a 6-digit code by email (valid 10 minutes). There is no password. Sessions last 12 hours.

## A case, step by step

1. **An intake arrives.** The partner's email to the intake address creates a case (reference `SPG-YYYY-NNNN`) and Claude fills in the details. You get an email "New case … needs review". Intakes that arrive another way: **New case**, optionally paste the text.
2. **Review and confirm.** Open the case. Amber fields were interpreted, red fields are missing. Correct everything, set the **date of signing**, and click **Confirm details and generate documents**. Nothing is generated or sent before this.
3. **Send for signature.** Check the four PDFs under *Documents* (Preview), then **Send for signature**. The client signs first in BoldSign, then you countersign (OA, Policy, Consent).
4. **Signed.** When both have signed, the signed PDFs are stored and the client automatically gets **email A** with the signed documents. If the signing date differs from the date printed in the documents, the case shows a warning; decide whether to regenerate and resend.
5. **Award Letter notary.** In *Pension Award Letter*: download the unsigned PDF, enter the meeting (tick the box to date the letter on the meeting day), sign it in the online notary meeting, then upload the notarized copy and the apostille (or forward the notary's email to the intake address with the reference in the subject and use **File as…** in *Inbound emails*). Send **email B** (you see a preview first) and record the courier under *Shipments*.
6. **First payment.** When the first pension payment has been made (usually 1–6 months later), click **Confirm first payment** and tick the confirmation. Only then can the certificate be generated, because it states that the pension "has been paid monthly since …".
7. **Certificate notary.** Same as step 5 in *First payment and Certificate*. Send **email C**: it carries the certificate and the complete set of all five documents. Record the courier.
8. **Close** the case.

## Good to know

- **Test mode:** while the yellow TEST MODE bar shows, every client email and the client's BoldSign request go to the test address instead of the client. The case log shows who it would have gone to.
- **Draft watermark:** until counsel approves the templates, all documents carry "DRAFT – PENDING COUNSEL REVIEW", and in live mode sending to clients is blocked.
- **Hold / Cancel** keep all data. **Delete case data** removes everything permanently (type the reference to confirm).
- **Unassigned inbox:** mail from unknown senders, or with an unknown reference, waits there. Create a case from it, attach it to a case, or dismiss it.
- **Reminders:** a daily check emails you about cases waiting too long (signature over 7 days, review over 2 days, notary over 14 days).
- Uploads: PDF, JPEG or PNG, up to 25 MB each.
